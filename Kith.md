# Kith

Kith are the creatures raised, trained, bonded with, and evolved by players in **DeltaPets**. A Kith is more than a battle unit: each individual can develop its own stats, elemental training, care history, bond, traits, memories, and communication with its trainer.

This page documents the current Kith design rules. Some late-game systems, especially breeding, advanced evolution, and Legendary content, are still being designed.

---

## Evolution Stages

The current full Kith evolution structure is:

1. **Egg**
2. **Hatchling**
3. **Lowform**
4. **Highform**
5. **Legion**
6. **Mythical Legendary** — optional for a species and intended as a later-game stage

Every species is expected to reach at least **Legion**. Not every species is required to have a Mythical Legendary form.

---

## Species Rarity

A Kith species has a permanent species rarity.

- **Common**
- **Uncommon**
- **Rare**
- **Epic**
- **Legendary** — planned for exceptional species/content

Species rarity describes **how rare the species itself is**.

A Common species does not become Rare because it rolled good stats. A very strong Pebelin is still a **Common Pebelin**.

Examples from the current Kithna roster:

- **Pebelin** — Common
- **Clodian** — Uncommon
- **Magmado** — Rare
- **Glimmer** — Epic

---

## Egg Families

Elemental eggs indicate the natural elemental family of the Kith developing inside.

Current elemental egg families include:

- **Tide** — Water
- **Ember** — Fire
- **Grove** — Earth
- **Zephyr** — Air
- **Frostveil** — Ice
- **Storm** — Storm
- **Dawnshard** — Light
- **Eclipse** — Shadow
- **Prismatic** — special starter egg

Voidborne Kith use their own special egg identity rather than a normal elemental family.

---

## Egg Rarity

Egg rarity and species rarity use the same rarity ladder, but the egg rarity describes **what rarity of species is developing inside the egg**.

- **Common Egg** → Common species
- **Uncommon Egg** → Uncommon species
- **Rare Egg** → Rare species
- **Epic Egg** → Epic species
- **Legendary Egg** → Legendary species, when Legendary content is introduced

Example:

> **Rare Grove Egg**
>
> Contains a **Rare Earth species**.

A Common Grove Egg does not suddenly hatch a Rare species. The rarity of the species and the rarity shown by its egg remain consistent.

---

## Egg Size

Egg **size** is separate from rarity.

Size represents how promising or unusually strong that **individual Kith** appears to be before hatching.

Current planned size tiers:

- **Normal**
- **Large**
- **Huge**
- **Colossal**

A Common species can therefore hatch from different-sized Common eggs.

Example:

- **Normal Common Grove Egg** → a normal Common Earth Kith
- **Large Common Grove Egg** → a stronger individual Common Earth Kith
- **Huge Common Grove Egg** → a very strong individual Common Earth Kith
- **Colossal Common Grove Egg** → an exceptionally promising individual Common Earth Kith

The species does **not** change rarity because the egg is larger.

### Visual Direction

As the egg becomes larger or more important, its artwork should remain recognizable as the same elemental egg family while becoming subtly more elaborate.

The intention is for Common through Legendary eggs to look related rather than like completely unrelated objects. Higher tiers may gain changes such as:

- larger physical size
- additional shell details
- stronger markings
- extra rocks, horns, cracks, or ornaments appropriate to the egg family
- stronger but still readable elemental effects

Legendary eggs should be visually special, but they are expected to be extremely uncommon and are not a tutorial expectation.

---

## Egg Base Stats

Each species has its own egg base-stat spread.

The egg base stat total is currently:

**10 total base points**

The six current main stats are:

- **HP**
- **ATK**
- **MAGI**
- **DEF**
- **SPD**
- **MANA**

These base stats represent the species' starting shape. Two eggs of the same species can share the same species base stats while still becoming different individuals when they hatch.

---

## Hatchling Bonus Points

When a Kith hatches, additional stat points are added to its egg base stats.

Current rarity bonuses are:

- **Common** — +7 hatch points
- **Uncommon** — +8 hatch points
- **Rare** — +9 hatch points
- **Epic** — +10 hatch points

The exact distribution of these hatch points can differ between individuals.

This means two Pebelin can begin with the same species egg base stats but hatch with different strengths.

Example:

One Pebelin may distribute more of its hatch points into HP and DEF, while another Pebelin may distribute more into ATK or SPD. Both are still Common Pebelin and both received the same total number of rarity-based hatch points.

There is **no single IV score** intended to tell players that one Kith is objectively perfect. Different stat distributions can support different builds.

---

## Growth Strengths and Weaknesses

Wild eggs can carry signs that the developing Kith is naturally stronger in certain stats or weaker in another.

These differences help make individual Kith feel distinct before player-controlled stat growth begins.

The Hatchery can inspect an egg's aura and describe these tendencies without needing to expose a single hidden "perfect IV" number.

Egg size can be used as a broad visual clue that an individual rolled especially promising growth characteristics.

---

## Player Stat Training

Beginning after the earliest hatchling stage, players become responsible for shaping their Kith's build by placing earned stat points themselves.

The intention is:

- the egg and hatch create an individual starting point
- the trainer decides how that Kith develops afterward

This allows two members of the same species to become very different builds.

---

## Traits and Mutations

Traits and mutations are individual characteristics and are separate from species rarity.

A Common species can possess a very rare trait or mutation without becoming a Rare, Epic, or Legendary species.

Example:

> **Pebelin**
>
> Species Rarity: Common
>
> Trait or Mutation: potentially much rarer

Trait and mutation rarity may use its own rarity ladder. This is independent from the Kith's species rarity.

A special mutation therefore means the **individual is unusual**, not that its species has changed rarity.

---

# Elements

## Natural Elements

Every normal Kith is born with at least **one Natural Element**.

Some species may naturally be born with **two Natural Elements**.

Natural Elements are part of the Kith from hatch and can affect:

- natural skill access
- egg appearance
- evolution requirements or paths
- future breeding inheritance

Examples:

> Natural Element: Earth

or

> Natural Elements: Water / Storm

A naturally dual-element Kith should still have a clear **primary elemental identity**. Its egg remains based primarily on that elemental egg family.

---

## Secondary Element Signs on Eggs

If a Kith is naturally dual-element, its egg should remain recognizable as the primary elemental egg while showing subtle signs of the second element.

Example:

> **Grove Egg**
>
> Primary: Earth
>
> Secondary: Storm

The egg may remain brown/green and clearly recognizable as a Grove Egg while showing subtle violet electrical cracks or Storm markings.

The second element should act as an accent rather than completely replacing the primary egg design.

---

## Elemental Training

Elements do **not** have Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, or Legendary rarity.

A trainer can develop additional elemental affinities through systems such as the Gym.

In the full game, a Kith can have up to:

**4 total elements**

A Kith may therefore develop from:

> Natural: Earth

into:

> Natural: Earth  
> Trained: Storm / Water / Light  
> Total: 4 / 4

A naturally dual-element Kith begins with two natural elements and therefore has room for up to two additional trained elements.

Trained elements do not rewrite the Kith's original egg or species identity.

---

## Element Skills

Kith have species-specific skills, but DeltaPets also supports general elemental skills that are **not limited to one species' personal skill sheet**.

These teachable skills can require specific elemental training.

Examples:

### 1-Element Skill

Requires one specific element:

> Earth

### 2-Element Skill

Requires two specific elements:

> Earth + Storm

### 3-Element Skill

Requires three specific elements:

> Water + Storm + Ice

### 4-Element Skill

Requires four specific elements:

> Earth + Water + Fire + Air

The Kith must possess every element required by the skill before it can use that skill.

A "4-element skill" describes its **element requirement**, not its rarity.

Elemental training is also intended to help influence later evolution choices.

---

# Breeding — Planned Rules

Breeding is a future system, but several rules are already important enough to document.

Natural Elements are intended to be inheritable.

The offspring can be born with no more than:

**2 Natural Elements**

A Kith can still train additional elements later until reaching the full-game maximum of four total elements.

Exact inheritance chances are **not finalized yet**.

Trained elements should not be treated as rarity. Future breeding design will decide how much a parent's training can influence an offspring's natural elemental inheritance.

Breeding is also expected to interact with:

- inherited stats
- traits and mutations
- stronger egg sizes
- longer hatch times for stronger offspring

The detailed breeding formula is intentionally being left open until the breeding system is built.

---

# Communication and Self-Awareness

Kith are intended to feel aware of the trainer and their surroundings rather than behaving like silent collectible objects.

The long-term design includes:

- Kith communication
- Kith language
- trainer/Kith understanding that improves over time
- individual memory
- Bond
- emotional reactions to care and treatment

These systems are being designed separately from the species stat sheet so that an individual Kith can develop its own relationship history.

---

## Kith Language and Runes

Kith initially communicate in **Kith language** rather than immediately speaking fully understandable human language.

The player can discover **Runes** that gradually improve their ability to understand their Kith.

The current design includes **26 Runes**.

Rune discovery and Bond are intended to work together so that communication becomes clearer over time instead of being fully translated from the beginning.

The Kith language itself still needs to be fully authored.

---

## Memory

Individual Kith are planned to have memories separate from their species template.

Memory can eventually allow a Kith to remember meaningful parts of its own life, such as interactions with its trainer, care, important events, and relationship history.

Memory is intended to belong to the **individual Kith**, not to every member of the species.

The exact memory storage and recall rules are still being designed.

---

## Bond

Bond represents the relationship between a trainer and an individual Kith.

Bond is intended to influence more than a number on a stats page. Planned uses include:

- communication and understanding
- emotional reactions
- trust
- care responses
- memories and relationship milestones
- evolution requirements

The detailed Bond progression system still needs to be fleshed out.

---

# Care and Mood

Kith have care needs and can react to the way they are treated.

Current care categories include:

- Hunger
- Cleanliness
- Happiness / Mood
- Comfort
- Rest
- Energy

Care is intended to be compatible with adult players who may not be able to check the game constantly.

Kith should be able to visibly respond when cared for. A Kith in a good mood may communicate happiness or appreciation, while a Kith in a poor mood may refuse an interaction or turn away from a care item.

Species personality, individual mood, Bond, and memory are intended to make these reactions feel different between Kith.

---

# Species Skills and Individual Builds

Each Kith species can have its own natural skill progression. The long-term plan is for species skill progression to extend through later levels, while general elemental skills exist as a separate teachable system.

This creates two complementary sources of skills:

1. **Species Skills** — skills that belong to the species' own progression
2. **Element Skills** — skills that can be taught when the Kith has the required elemental training

Special systems may later provide additional unique, mutation, event, Bond, or evolution skills.

---

# Important Separation of Systems

The following systems should not be treated as the same thing:

| System | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Species Rarity** | How rare the species is |
| **Egg Rarity** | What rarity of species is developing inside |
| **Egg Size** | How promising/strong the individual egg appears |
| **Trait / Mutation Rarity** | How unusual an individual trait is |
| **Natural Elements** | Elements the Kith is born with |
| **Trained Elements** | Additional elemental affinities developed later |
| **Element Skill Requirement** | Specific 1–4 element combination required for a teachable skill |

A Kith can therefore be a **Common species**, hatch from a **Common egg**, have an unusually **large egg**, possess a very rare **trait**, and later train several additional **elements** without any of those systems changing the meaning of the others.
